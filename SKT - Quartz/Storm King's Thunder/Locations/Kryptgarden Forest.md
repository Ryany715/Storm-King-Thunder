# Kryptgarden Forest

## Overview
A forest home to Old Gnawbone, an ancient and powerful green dragon that Chazlauth Yarghorn hopes to recruit as an ally against the giant threat. The forest is dotted with strange magical curiosities and territorial dangers as one travels deeper toward the dragon's domain.

## Unique Features & Landmarks

### 1. The Gentleman's Tower
*   **Description:** A collapsed stone tower featuring a doorframe with a magical "reflection" of whoever approaches, blocking the way. Passing requires stepping aside and saying something polite like "after you!" — the reflection will respond in kind, step aside, and vanish once the real party passes through.
*   **Significance:** Behind the door, past a translucent magical mirror, sits an ornate wooden chest containing a Mirror of the Past. The surrounding stone has collapsed, barring forced entry without a high Strength or Dexterity check.
*   **Plot Hooks:** An optional/alternate travel encounter (d20 roll of 1–10) not yet used as of Session 8.

### 2. The Mushroom Venn Diagram
*   **Description:** Interlapping circles of mushrooms forming a fairy-ring triad, found by the party (per the d20 roll of 11–20, or simply encountered directly in play).
*   **Significance:** Standing in the center of each ring and placing a shared item or memory at each intersection lights up the applicable sections and yields a Mirror of the Past. The party found a bottle of smoke here.
*   **Plot Hooks:** The exact nature/use of the recovered bottle of smoke is still unresolved.

### 3. Old Gnawbone's Domain
*   **Description:** The deeper reaches of the forest, home to the ancient green dragon [[Old Gnawbone]].
*   **Significance:** Central goal of the party's current expedition into the forest, seeking her aid against the giants.
*   **Plot Hooks:** Two Bullywog frog sages prowl the very edge of her territory — an available but not-yet-used encounter.

### A Wandering Hill Giant
*   **Type:** Martial
*   **Manifestation:** A hill giant roams the forest searching for someone named "Moog," questioning travelers and attacking if they prove unhelpful.

*   **Why hasn't it been dealt with?** The party instead convinced him to challenge Old Gnawbone directly, resulting in his death.
*   **Key Players:** The unnamed hill giant, [[Old Gnawbone]]
