## Background
Morak became the de facto leader of Nightstone in the wake of Lady Velrosa's death, organizing the escape and safety of the town's citizens during the goblin/worg crisis. Upon the party's return with the rescued villagers, Morak thanked them and hosted a night of celebration.

## Motivations & Goals
To stabilize and protect Nightstone in the absence of formal leadership, and to maintain the town's outside relationships (such as with the Xelbrin family of Goldenfields).
