# Zephyros

## Description
*   **Appearance:** A cloud giant residing within a massive tower made of clouds.
*   **Personality:** Speaks in strange, meandering sentences about what is and will be — cryptic, seer-like.
*   **Mannerisms:** Prone to lore-dumping and philosophical tangents; welcomes guests warmly, including hosting formal dinners.

## Background
Zephyros descended upon the party in his cloud tower shortly after they departed Nightstone. He explained that he has seen visions of the party and believes they may be "the chosen" meant to help reshape the shattered Ordning. He introduced the party to Odette, a stone giant he indicated belonged with the group, and hosted them for dinner, during which he shared extensive lore about giant society and history.

## Homebrew Role & Edits
*Per the DM's story edits: Zephyros acts primarily as a guide in this campaign. Upon first encounter, the party is meant to be heading toward Goldenfields, railroaded there by Morak (who only offers the Goldenfields quest). Zephyros's demeanor remains as written, but he is willing to divulge the following lore:*

1.  Giant society is defined by the **Ordning**, a caste system imposed by the god of giants, Annam the All-Father.
2.  The Ordning ranks giant-kind as follows (highest to lowest):
    1.  **Storm Giants** — Powerful seers.
    2.  **Cloud Giants** — Extravagant and aristocratic.
    3.  **Fire Giants** — Warmongering smiths and crafters.
    4.  **Frost Giants** — Martial prowess and might.
    5.  **Stone Giants** — Stone-sculpting and creativity.
    6.  **Hill Giants** — The biggest rule.
3.  Since the Ordning's shattering, giant-kind has been pitted against itself, with lords of each caste rising up to vie for power.
4.  **King Hekaton**, ruler of the storm giants, long kept giant-kind in check, curtailing evil and malignant giants from raiding small-folk settlements. Shortly after the Ordning shattered, Hekaton disappeared, leaving his court in disarray. Zephyros has heard rumors that his youngest daughter, **Serissa**, now sits upon the Wyrmskull Throne.
5.  Zephyros believes King Hekaton's might could help re-establish order among giant-kind and prevent full-scale war.
6.  Zephyros is setting out to learn more about each giant lord, but as of the party's first meeting with him, knows only of **Brokh**, who has been "eating his way across the open fields."

## Motivations & Goals
To identify and understand each of the rising giant lords, and to help guide events (and the party) toward re-establishing order under the Ordning — potentially by restoring King Hekaton's authority.
