Session #10 Prep: Waterdeep — Petitioning the Watchful Order
## Strong Start  
  
The players spend the night in the yawning portal, where: 
1. A wizard's apprentice named Jonathan Sparkles who is attempting the map out the yawning portal at the behest of the watchful order of magistrates and protectors
2. Jonathan will approach the players to offer them the hospitality of the The Order, saying they are paying for their food and drink for the night 
3. He will detail he has been tasked with mapping out some portions of the Yawning portal
	1. He is currently in the process of mapping out a long abandoned forge that belonged to an ancient Dwarven clan by the name of Dugeddin. 
	2. The order has heard tell that there may lie some old legendary cache of weapons there

  ## The Watchful Order Meeting 

1. The order is concerned about the recent giant uprising and very interested in Zephyros and his flying tower
2. The Watchful Order regulates spellcasting in Waterdeep and has its own interests to protect; committing battle mages to a rural town's giant problem is a real commitment, not a rubber stamp. The Order expects a future discount on incoming agrarian goods to offset the cost of supplying mages to the cause and protection of Golden Fields
3.  Canon reveals Naxene is secretly a Lords' Alliance spy loyal to **Lady Laeral Silverhand**, the current Open Lord of Waterdeep. Naxene will push the ask up the food chain to force the Order to supply help if needed, the protection of the Goldenfields is paramount to Lareal SIlverhand. 

After successful completion of the Order meetin, or exploration of the yawning portal, the players will be level 5. 
## A Cloud Giant Castle Over Waterdeep

Shortly after arriving in Waterdeep, a cloud giant castle emerges from the clouds and looms over the city, panicking the populace before Lady Laeral's heralds calm things down. The castle belongs to a neutral-good cloud giant clan — not hostile by default. Entirely optional — use it if you want a mid-session jolt, skip it if you'd rather keep this session focused on the Watchful Order.

  The order knows of this ancient temple the Eye of the allfather. There is rumor that the place houses divine knowledge that the giant lords of old hid away. If the players divulge any information about what Old Gnawbone has said, the Order will insist that the party travel to the north to find the secrets within, it's very possible it could aid in the protection of the Goldenfields. Jonathan will also know about this.
