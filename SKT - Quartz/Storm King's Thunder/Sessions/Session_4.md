# Session #4: Arrival in Goldenfields

## Recap from Last Session
*   The party dined with Zephyros aboard his cloud tower, learning of the Ordning's history and the war between giants and dragons.
*   Odette, a stone giant, joined the party.
*   Zephyros believes the party may be "the chosen" meant to help reshape the Ordning.

## Session Overview
The next day, the party encountered humans riding giant vultures — cultists of Yan-C-Bin who had come to meet with Zephyros. Through creative problem-solving, the players convinced the cultist leader of the error of his ways, leading him to consider repenting and worshiping Silvanus instead. The tower then touched down in the sprawling agricultural land of Goldenfields, and the party made their way into the walled city dedicated to the goddess Chauntea, spotting the stone building known as the Earth Mother's Bounty.
