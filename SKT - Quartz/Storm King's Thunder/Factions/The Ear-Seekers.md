## Background
An orc raiding party from the forest near Nightstone. They besieged the town shortly after the goblin/worg crisis, intending to hole up within it, but were convinced by Snave to instead pursue rumored "treasure" to the north at the Dripping Caves. There, they clashed with the goblins holding the Nightstone villagers captive, allying briefly with the party against their shared goblin enemy before ultimately being subdued themselves.

## Motivations & Goals
Originally sought to raid and occupy Nightstone; shifted focus to claiming the Dripping Caves and any treasure within.
