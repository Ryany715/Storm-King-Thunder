# Session #3: Departure & the Cloud Tower

## Recap from Last Session
*   The party helped free the Nightstone villagers from the Dripping Caves and subdued the Ear-Seekers' leaders.
*   Pojo was crowned the new goblin boss.

## Session Overview
The party reunited with the rescued Nightstone citizens and met Morak UrGray, the town's de facto leader, who had organized their escape. After warning Morak of the Zhentarim's presence and celebrating the villagers' return, the party was tasked with delivering news of a death in the prominent Xelbrin family of Goldenfields. Gnash left the party via a note, her mercenary contract complete. Setting out as a duo, Snave and Solliel encountered a massive descending cloud tower belonging to Zephyros, a strange cloud giant, who introduced them to Odette, a stone giant meant to join their group, and shared a dinner discussing the Ordning and the history of the giants.

## Key Events & Discoveries

### Scene 1: Reunion & Morak UrGray
*   The party caught up with the Nightstone citizens and spoke with [[Morak UrGray]], the de facto leader of Nightstone, who had orchestrated the villagers' escape and safety.
*   The party told Morak about the [[The Zhentarim]] agents who had taken up residence in town.
*   The town celebrated the return of the mostly intact villagers that night.

### Scene 2: Morak's Quest & Gnash's Departure
*   The next morning, Morak tasked the party with delivering news of the death of a member of the Xelbrin family, a prominent family in [[Goldenfields]].
*   As the party readied to depart, they found a note from Gnash explaining she would no longer be traveling with them, her mercenary task complete.

### Scene 3: Zephyros's Cloud Tower
*   The remaining duo, forlorn at Gnash's departure, set out from Nightstone.
*   A massive tower made of clouds descended toward them.
*   Inside, they found a strange cloud giant named [[Zephyros]], who spoke in meandering sentences about what is and will be.
![[Zephyros.png]]
*   A strange, pallid stone giant descended from the tower as well; Zephyros indicated this giant, [[Odette]], belonged with the party.
*   Zephyros explained he had spoken with powerful beings of other planes who suggested the players could be who he was looking for.
![[Cloud Tower.png]]
### Scene 4: Dinner with Zephyros
*   Zephyros welcomed the players into his tower and invited them to dinner.
*   Over the meal, he discussed the Ordning and its shattering, and answered questions about giant history, including Annam the All-Father and the war with Tiamat, queen of the chromatic dragons.
