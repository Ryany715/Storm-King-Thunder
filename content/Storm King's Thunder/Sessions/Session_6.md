# Session #6: The Siege of Goldenfields

## Recap from Last Session
*   The Goldenfields council meeting was interrupted by alarm bells.
*   Evidence at the city wall breach linked a prior giant attack to Brokh the Hill Giant King.
## Session Overview
A tall, emaciated hill giant led an attack on Goldenfields, sending goblins, bugbears, and hill giants against the city. The party teamed up with the local guard, saving both Captain Thunderblade and the city itself. Afterward, the council decided to seek aid from the Watchful Order of Magists and Protectors in Waterdeep, with Naxene agreeing to escort the party and insisting they first visit Chazlauth Yarghorn, a dragon expert. Before departing, the party's druid visited the Emerald Enclave grove, meeting an awakened treant named Lifferlas, who agreed to help rebuild the broken wall and shared word of the giant-killer druid Aerglas.

## Key Events & Discoveries

### Scene 1: The Attack on Goldenfields
*   A tall, emaciated hill giant with wispy, oily black hair — later revealed to be [[Brokh the Hill Giant King]] himself — led an attack on Goldenfields.
*   Brokh sent a myriad of goblins, bugbears, and hill giants against the city, including the Watchful Order of Magists and Protectors' position within it.
*   The party teamed up with the local guard, managing to save both Captain [[Strog Thunderblade]] and the city of Goldenfields.

### Scene 2: Aftermath & the Call to Waterdeep
*   The party met with the city council to discuss next steps, deciding to parlay with the [[Watchful Order of Magists and Protectors]] in Waterdeep for additional aid, in the form of battle mages to protect Goldenfields from future hill giant assaults.
*   [[Naxene Drathkala]] agreed to escort the party to Waterdeep, and insisted they visit a mage there named [[Chazlauth Yarghorn]], a dragon expert with insight into the ancient feud between giants and dragons.
*   [[Zi Liang]] asked the party to bring a pearl to her family.

### Scene 3: The Emerald Enclave
*   Before departing Goldenfields, the party's druid went to visit the grove of the [[Emerald Enclave]].
*   There, she met [[Lifferlas]], an awakened treant, who agreed to help reconstruct the broken wall protecting Goldenfields.
*   Lifferlas told her of the druid who awakened him, named [[Aerglas]], famed as a giant-killer, who has made a pilgrimage to Shadowtop Cathedral to speak with [[Turlang]], the oldest treant in the Emerald Enclave.
