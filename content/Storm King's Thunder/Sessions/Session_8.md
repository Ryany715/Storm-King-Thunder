# Session #8: Journey to Old Gnawbone

## Recap from Last Session
*   The party reached the apex of Chazlauth Yarghorn's tower and met him for the first time, accompanied by his small silver dragon.
## Session Overview
Chazlauth detailed the ancient history of giants and dragons, including the great war and the founding of the Ordning in its aftermath. He proposed that good-aligned dragons might help curb the giants' recent aggression, specifically a dragon named Old Gnawbone in Kryptgarden Forest. After some convincing, Chazlauth agreed to accompany the party, teleporting them directly to the forest. There, he performed a strange steeling ritual on himself before the party solved a puzzle involving interlocking rings of mushrooms, discovering a bottle of smoke at their center.

## Key Events & Discoveries

### Scene 1: The History of Giants and Dragons
*   [[Chazlauth Yarghorn]] told the players of the extensively researched history between giants and dragons.
*   It is rumored giants were the first mortal beings to walk the realms, with dragons — led by the goddess Tiamat — arriving shortly after.
*   A long war between the species ensued, ending in the dragons' victory.
*   In the war's wake, the giants established societal stability under a new order: the Ordning.

### Scene 2: Chazlauth's Proposal
*   Chazlauth believes recent giant attacks on small folk might be assuaged by securing the aid of good-aligned dragons, who may still harbor resentment toward the giants from the ancient war.
*   He mentioned a dragon named Old Gnawbone, who lives in Kryptgarden Forest.
*   After some convincing, Chazlauth agreed to accompany the players, teleporting the apex of his tower along with the party directly to the forest.

### Scene 3: Arrival in Kryptgarden Forest
*   Upon arrival, Chazlauth conducted a strange ritual on himself, seemingly steeling his resolve, before urging the party to continue on deeper into the forest.
*   The players came across a strange venn-diagram arrangement of mushroom circles, which lit up various objects when interacted with.
*   The party solved the mushroom puzzle and found a strange bottle of smoke at its center.
