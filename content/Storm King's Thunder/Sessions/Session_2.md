# Session #2: The Dripping Caves

## Recap from Last Session
*   The party redirected the Ear-Seekers orcs toward the Dripping Caves rather than let them sack Nightstone.
*   Pojo, a captured goblin, was sent ahead to attempt to free the captive villagers.
*   The Nightstone guards agreed to march north to the caves alongside the party.

## Session Overview
The players followed the Ear-Seekers orcs north to the Dripping Caves and found them already fighting the goblins holding the village captives. Allying with the orcs, the party helped defeat the goblins, freed the villagers, and watched the orcs storm deeper into the cave to defeat Boss Hark and his minions. With the goblins cleared, only the party, Pojo, Gurrash the orc warchief, and Norga One-Eye, his shaman, remained. The party then turned on the weakened orc leaders, subduing them, and found a disappointingly small treasure in Boss Hark's hoard before leaving the cave — with Pojo now the newly crowned goblin boss.
