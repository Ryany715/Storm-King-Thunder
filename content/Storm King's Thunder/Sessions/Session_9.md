# Session #9: Old Gnawbone

## Recap from Last Session
*   The party, guided by a teleporting Chazlauth Yarghorn, arrived in Kryptgarden Forest and solved a mushroom-ring puzzle, finding a strange bottle of smoke.

## Session Overview
Deeper in the forest, the party came across a hill giant roaming in search of someone named Moog. Through clever persuasion, the players convinced him to challenge Old Gnawbone in her own domain — a challenge he did not survive. The green dragon descended from the skies and killed the giant before turning her attention to the party, who then spoke with her directly. Gnawbone details to the players that they must travel north and search for a giant temple called the Eye of the All-Father. "Therein you'll learn what must be done to end the giant menace." She tells them as well to keep an eye out for a frost giant wearing a helm made from a white dragon's skull, as he can aid them. The players accompany Chazlauth back to Waterdeep in his tower, and make their way to the yawning portal, where they engage in a bit of revelry and fun, with Snave putting on a show for the whole bar.
