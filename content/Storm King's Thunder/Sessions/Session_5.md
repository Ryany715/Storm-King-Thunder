# Session #5: Exploring Goldenfields

## Recap from Last Session
*   The party arrived in Goldenfields via Zephyros's tower and spotted the Earth Mother's Bounty near the gatehouse.
*   The party still needs to deliver Morak's news to the Xelbrin family.

## Session Overview
The party began exploring Goldenfields with Odette, making their way to North Furrow's End, the tavern owned by Miros Xelbrin. In delivering the news of his family member's death, Miros rewarded the party with a magic ring and was warned in turn about Brokh the Hill Giant King's rampage across the Great Plains. Miros called a meeting of the Goldenfields council for that night. While waiting, the party split up: Solliel found the Harvesthome Abbey and its resident bears, Odette investigated a breach in the city wall and a dead giant's corpse, and Snave met Naxene Drathkala of the Watchful Order of Magists and Protectors. The council meeting was cut short by the town's alarm bells.

## Key Events & Discoveries

### Scene 1: North Furrow's End & Miros Xelbrin
*   The party explored Goldenfields with their new ally, Odette.
*   They made their way to North Furrow's End, the tavern owned by [[Miros Xelbrin]], otherwise known as the Yeti.
*   In delivering the news of his dead family member, Miros was appreciative and gave the players a magic ring as payment.
*   The players warned Miros of the encroaching threat they had just learned about: [[Brokh the Hill Giant King]], who has been laying waste to and sieging across the Great Plains.
*   Miros decided it best to call a meeting with the Goldenfields council for that night.

### Scene 2: The Party Splits
*   While waiting for the meeting, the party split up to explore the town.
*   Solliel discovered the Harvesthome Abbey, finding two old black bears named Darlowe and Tillbee.
*   Odette explored a breach in the Goldenfields wall, created by a previous hill giant attack, and examined the corpse of a dead giant, finding a strange articulating circular bladed device and a brand shaped like a crude "B" on the back of the giant's skull.
*   Snave met with [[Naxene Drathkala]], the Waterdeep Watchful Order of Magists and Protectors attaché. Naxene was, surprisingly, a fan of Snave's work, and explained her research on giant history and the recent attacks. Snave beseeched her help in requesting aid from Waterdeep to combat Brokh.

### Scene 3: The Council Meeting
*   The party reconvened for the council meeting, developing a plan to use Zone of Truth to ensure all attendees were being honest.
*   Present at the meeting were [[Miros Xelbrin]], [[Naxene Drathkala]], [[Zi Liang]], [[Ellardin]], and [[Strog Thunderblade]] — the latter of whom resisted the Zone of Truth spell.
*   The council discussed the need for Waterdeep's assistance regarding Brokh and his hill giants; accusations and arguments were exchanged.
*   The meeting was cut short by the sound of the town's alarm bells ringing out across Goldenfields.
