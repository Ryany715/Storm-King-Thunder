*   Naxene Drathkala agreed to escort the party to Waterdeep, insisting they first visit Chazlauth Yarghorn.

## Session Overview
The party traveled to Waterdeep in the company of Naxene Drathkala. Upon arrival, she directed them to seek out Chazlauth Yarghorn in the northern district while she debriefed the Watchful Order ahead of an upcoming meeting. The party found Chazlauth's dragon-and-giant-engraved tower and successfully navigated its puzzles and traps, eventually reaching the apex where Chazlauth awaited them with a small silver dragon perched on his shoulder.
