# Homebrew: The Shattered Ordning

## Overview
This page consolidates the DM's homebrew edits to Storm King's Thunder as written. It ties together the general concept of the Ordning, its shattering, and the five edited Giant Lords driving the campaign's central conflict. For lore, encounter, and location detail specific to each individual Giant Lord, see their respective NPC and location pages.

## The Baseline (As Written)
Storm King's Thunder, as written, has the player characters helping the people of the Savage Frontier against 5 Giant Lords who are ravaging the land in the wake of the shattering of the Ordning. The traditional hierarchy of giants has been broken by the giant god, and in the chaos, [[King Hekaton]] — current King of the Storm Giants, and top of the Ordning — has disappeared to investigate the murder of his beloved wife. The 5 Giant Lords of each caste are using the unrest of the shattering to further their own personal agendas, some for the good of their people, and some for personal gain.

## The Homebrew Edits
The as-written Giant Lords have been edited to create a more compelling set of antagonists for the PCs to engage with. Additionally, they've been edited with the intention that the PCs engage with **all** of them, significantly increasing the campaign's scope and length. The court of the Storm Giants remains as-is. The changes to each Giant Lord are meant to make their antagonistic plans more engaging, effectively creating a series of sub-campaigns that reinforce the overarching goal: helping to re-establish the Ordning and aiding the Storm Giants once the party becomes sufficiently notorious among giant-kind.

## The Ordning (Caste Structure)
Giant society is defined by the Ordning, a caste system imposed by the god of giants, Annam the All-Father. From highest to lowest:

1.  **Storm Giants** — Powerful seers.
2.  **Cloud Giants** — Extravagant and aristocratic.
3.  **Fire Giants** — Warmongering smiths and crafters.
4.  **Frost Giants** — Martial prowess and might.
5.  **Stone Giants** — Stone-sculpting and creativity.
6.  **Hill Giants** — The biggest rule.

Since the Ordning's shattering, giant-kind has turned against itself, with a lord of each caste rising to vie for power. [[King Hekaton]]'s disappearance removed the one figure capable of curtailing evil, malignant giants from raiding small-folk settlements — his youngest daughter, Serissa, is rumored to now sit the Wyrmskull Throne in his stead.

## Campaign Structure Notes
*   The party's early arc (Chapters 1–2 as written) was compressed: after Nightstone, they were deliberately "railroaded" toward Goldenfields via Morak's quest and Zephyros's tower, since Morak only offers the Goldenfields hook.
*   Zephyros is the primary vehicle for slowly revealing each Giant Lord's identity and plans as the campaign progresses.
*   The intended endgame ties back to re-establishing the Ordning and restoring (or replacing) King Hekaton's authority.
