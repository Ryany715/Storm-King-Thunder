# Chazlauth Yarghorn

## Description
*   **Appearance:** A wizard often accompanied by a small silver dragon perched on his shoulder.
*   **Personality:** Scholarly and thorough — has done extensive personal research on the history of giants and dragons.
*   **Mannerisms:** Performs a strange, resolve-steeling ritual on himself before entering dangerous territory (as seen before entering Kryptgarden Forest).

## Background
Chazlauth resides at the apex of his puzzle-filled tower in Waterdeep's northern district (see [[Chazlauth's Tower]]). Recommended by Naxene Drathkala, the party braved his tower's traps and puzzles to reach him. Chazlauth has extensively researched the ancient history between giants and dragons, and believes recruiting good-aligned dragons — who may still harbor resentment toward giants from their ancient war — could help curb the current giant threat. He agreed to accompany the party to Kryptgarden Forest to seek out Old Gnawbone, teleporting his tower's apex there directly.

## Motivations & Goals
*   To use his knowledge of giant-dragon history to help broker alliances with good-aligned dragons against the rising giant threat.
*   Personally invested in seeking out Old Gnawbone specifically.

## DM Notes
### Lore Delivered to the Party
Millennia ago, giants and dragons warred for dominance over the realms. It's rumored giants were the first mortal beings to walk the realms, with dragons — led by Tiamat — arriving soon after to claim the realms for themselves. What followed was a long, bloody war, ultimately won by the dragons. In its wake, giant-kind's ancient societal structure was destroyed, giving rise to the new order: the Ordning.

Chazlauth believes that as centuries passed, this ancient war curdled into deep-seated animosity between the races. He is increasingly worried by recent reports of giant-kind decimating small folk across the realm, and believes good-aligned dragons — some of whom may still remember the war — could provide crucial information on defeating the giants, or even lend direct aid.

He has heard of a dragon named **Old Gnawbone**, who lives in Kryptgarden Forest and is rumored to use scrying crystal balls to watch over "everyone and anything of note." He believes she won't harm the party so long as they are sincerely intent on helping against the giant threat.

Chazlauth has also heard of the wider giant lord crisis: Brokh the Hill Giant King laying siege to the Goldenfields, **Thane Sollielalithica** (a stone giant rumored to be seeking to "cleanse the dream world"), and **Jarl Storvald** (who seeks to bring about an age of everlasting ice). He believes there may be a giant lord from each caste vying for power as part of some larger, terrible plan.
