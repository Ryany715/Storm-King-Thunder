# Brokh the Hill Giant King

## Description
*   **Appearance:** An emaciated and sinewy hill giant, standing a few heads taller than the bloated giants around him, with wispy, oily strands of long black hair. Notably lithe and malformed compared to typical hill giants.
*   **Personality:** Intelligent and calculating beneath his monstrous hunger — unusually sapient for a hill giant, having "broken" into unnatural intellect through years of maddening starvation.
*   **Mannerisms:** Ritualistically starves himself between feasts, believing it deepens his mind; savors the act of consuming intelligent minds as both nourishment and pleasure.

## Background
Brokh was born a hill giant runt — sickly, rashy, and frail — yet voracious, quickly outgrowing his peers through sheer gluttony. In young adulthood, Annam the All-Father cursed him with an insatiable hunger, seeing in Brokh a potential champion of the hill giants who could rise through the Ordning. When the Ordning shattered, Brokh found he could no longer satiate his hunger no matter how much he consumed. His tribe's shaman saw this as a horrible omen and had him bound and starved in an attempt to cleanse him of the curse. Years of enforced hunger drove Brokh mad — and in that madness, his psyche broke into an unnatural intellect and sapience he'd never possessed before.

Brokh escaped his binds and rampaged across the Goldenfields region, gorging on any creature he found. In his gluttony, he discovered a new pleasure: consuming sentient, intelligent creatures, savoring the "sweet taste" of their intellect. He now ritualistically starves himself between feasts, believing the practice deepens his mind, breaking his fasts only to feast on the brains of small folk and other civilized, intelligent beings.

Brokh has amassed a large herd of hill giants who follow him for his ability to keep them fed and equitably provisioned. He preaches the raiding of small-folk villages, first offering any survivors the chance to adopt his cannibalistic practices; those who agree join his herd as human/small-folk cannibal ritualists known as the **[[Skull Crackers]]**. Brokh now ravages the Goldenfields, amassing both hill giants and Skull Crackers into a roving, Mongol-like warband intent on pillaging and corrupting the settlements around them. He craves the corruption of the civilized and the consumption of the largest, most intelligent minds he can find — reveling equally in conversion and consumption.

Left unchecked, Brokh will eventually consume the Goldenfields entirely, and may even besiege Waterdeep itself given enough time.

## Motivations & Goals
*   To feed his unending, cursed hunger by consuming intelligent and civilized minds.
*   To grow his herd of hill giants and Skull Crackers through conquest and cannibalistic conversion.
*   Ultimately, to overrun the Goldenfields and potentially threaten Waterdeep.

## DM Notes
### Storm King's Thunder Story Edit — First Encounter
The PCs first encounter Brokh in Chapter 2 of SKT, "railroaded" there immediately after Chapter 1 via Zephyros's flying cloud tower. The first true encounter is with members of Brokh's herd attacking Goldenfields — a scouting party assessing the city's defenses and capabilities. During this siege, the players see a small herd of simple, brutish, disorganized hill giants attacking, while the malformed, oily-haired outline of Brokh watches from a hill above. This encounter closely follows the module as written, except the players only need to defeat 2 of the 6 squadrons laying waste to the city. Brokh then leads his army back over the hill, seeking to strike fear rather than press the full assault — Goldenfields residents report that Brokh's tribe has been attacking at a near-random cadence, sowing discomfort and unrest.
