# Old Gnawbone

## Description
*   **Appearance:** An ancient green dragon.
*   **Personality:** Playful in a predatory way — "prowls and prods the party like a cat playing with her prey."
*   **Mannerisms:** Rumored to possess crystal balls she uses to scry on "everyone and anything of note."

## Background
Old Gnawbone is an ancient dragon residing in Kryptgarden Forest, sought out by Chazlauth Yarghorn as a potential ally against the giant threat. The party witnessed her swiftly kill a hill giant who challenged her in her own domain (a challenge the party themselves engineered), before she turned to speak with them directly.

## Motivations & Goals
Unclear — she may harbor lingering resentment toward giants from the ancient giant-dragon war, per Chazlauth's theory, but her true intentions toward the party remain to be seen.
