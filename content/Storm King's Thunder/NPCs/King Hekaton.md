## Background
King Hekaton, ruler of the storm giants and top of the Ordning hierarchy, long kept giant-kind in check, curtailing evil and malignant giants from raiding small-folk settlements. Shortly after the Ordning shattered, Hekaton disappeared to investigate the murder of his beloved wife, leaving his court in disarray. Zephyros has heard rumors that Hekaton's youngest daughter, Serissa, now sits upon the Wyrmskull Throne in his absence.
