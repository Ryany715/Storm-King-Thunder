## Background
Pojo was a goblin captured and interrogated by the party during the crisis in Nightstone. Through bribery and coercion, they convinced him to head to the Dripping Caves and free the villagers being held captive by [[Boss Hark]]'s warband. He appears to have followed through, and by the end of the Dripping Caves conflict, was crowned the new goblin boss in Hark's place.

## Motivations & Goals
Originally self-preservation; now presumably to maintain his new position as goblin boss.
