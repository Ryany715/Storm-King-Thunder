## Background
Gurrash is the orcish warchief of the Ear-Seekers, a raiding band redirected from Nightstone toward the Dripping Caves by Snave. After allying with the party to defeat the goblins holding the Nightstone villagers, Gurrash and his shaman Norga One-Eye were ultimately turned on by the party, subdued with a combination of magic (watery flowers) and misdirection, rather than killed.

## Motivations & Goals
Originally sought to raid Nightstone, then to claim the treasure of the Dripping Caves.
