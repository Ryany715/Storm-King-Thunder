## Background
Snave is a reporter who works for the Star Gazette in Baldur's Gate. He arrived in [[Nightstone]] as part of a travelling caravan, originally seeking an interview with Lady Velrosa and a report on the town.

## Motivations & Goals
*   Originally sought a story on Lady Velrosa and Nightstone.
*   Has since become entangled in the growing giant crisis, beseeching aid from the [[Watchful Order of Magists and Protectors]] via [[Naxene Drathkala]] on behalf of Goldenfields.
