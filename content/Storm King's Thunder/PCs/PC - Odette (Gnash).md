## Background
Gnash began the campaign as a sellsword for hire, sent to help Lady Velrosa in [[Nightstone]]. After completing her contracted task, Gnash left the party behind via a note, her work as a mercenary complete.

Shortly after, the party encountered [[Zephyros]]'s cloud tower descending from the sky. A strange, pallid stone giant — freed from the shattered Ordning — descended from the tower alongside Zephyros, who indicated she belonged with the group. This stone giant, Odette, joined the party in Gnash's place.

## Motivations & Goals
*   As Odette, seeks to understand the "waking dream" of the surface world.
