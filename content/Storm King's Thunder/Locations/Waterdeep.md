# Waterdeep

![[City_Image.png]]

## Overview
The great city of Waterdeep, largely run as written in Storm King's Thunder, with one notable homebrew addition: an expanded, puzzle-filled tower belonging to the dragon expert Chazlauth Yarghorn in the northern district.

## Unique Features & Landmarks

### 1. The Yawning Portal
*   **Description:** A well-known tavern within the city.
*   **Significance:** Naxene Drathkala's designated meeting point with the party after their visit to Chazlauth.
*   **Plot Hooks:** Potential future meeting or rest point.

### 2. Chazlauth Yarghorn's Tower
*   **Description:** A large stone cylinder in the northern district, adorned with embossments of dragons and giants locked in battle. See [[Chazlauth's Tower]] for full floor-by-floor detail.
*   **Significance:** Home to Chazlauth Yarghorn, a dragon expert whose knowledge is key to the party's efforts against the giants.
*   **Plot Hooks:** The tower's many puzzles and traps could hide further secrets beyond what's been explored.
