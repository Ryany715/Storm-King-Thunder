# Chazlauth's Tower

## Overview
A large stone cylinder in the northern district of Waterdeep, embossed with images of dragons and giants locked in eternal battle. The tower is a fully homebrewed 9-floor puzzle dungeon leading to Chazlauth Yarghorn's study at its apex. Notably, all 9 floors are windowless, and the tower's interior is far larger than its exterior would suggest.

## Unique Features & Landmarks

### 1. The Dragon's Door (Front Entrance)
*   **Description:** The front door is locked by an arcane lock spell and shaped like a red dragon with its mouth agape. A DC 10 Investigation check of the door reveals soot and ash inside the dragon's mouth, and a small opening/hole below the head — the blackened portions and soot are on the inside of the mouth.
*   **Significance:** The primary means of entry to the tower.
*   **Plot Hooks:** The door can be unlocked via a *knock* spell, or by placing any means of fire into the small hole, which causes the dragon to ignite and spit fire.

## Floor-by-Floor Breakdown

### Floor 1: Entrance
A fairly mildly furnished room that looks rather worn down. Not much to see beyond a staircase going up to Floor 2.

### Floor 2: The Lone Table Room
Simply furnished with a small bookshelf and a single table holding two books: *A History of Good Decisions* and *Big Book of Magic*. The book of decisions repeats the phrase "Thou shalt not enter a wizard's home uninvited" on every page. The book of magic simply repeats the word "Magic" over and over.

Upon entering, the stairways up and down both become sealed. A mirror on the wall shows a reflection of the room. A DC 12 Investigation check of the mirror reveals that the book on the table and a book on the bookshelf are swapped in the reflection. Swapping the real books to match unlocks the staircases.

*(Continuing up from here leads to Floor 5; going down leads to Floor 3 — see the tower's internal, non-linear floor connections below.)*

### Floor 3: Web Room
This room is covered in spider webs. Going up leads to Floor 2; going down leads to Floor 4.

### Floor 4: Tree Room
A massive tree grows out of the center of the stone floor, rising to the ceiling. The ground is covered in strange blue leaves.

A locked door blocks the staircase upward (marked "Floor 5" in thieves' cant above it), secured with **seven locks**, each inscribed in a different language, plus a thick iron turning handle in the center of the door:

| Lock | Language | Instruction |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Common | "Open" |
| 2 | Elvish | "Close" |
| 3 | Dwarvish | "Open" |
| 4 | Infernal | "Close" |
| 5 | Giant | "Open" |
| 6 | Halfling | "Close" |
| 7 | Undercommon | "Close" |

**Puzzle objective:** Unlock the locks marked "Open" (DC 10, or a trap triggers) and leave the locks marked "Close" untouched, then turn the door handle. Failing this activates a Lightning Rune in the center of the hallway (or another trap of the DM's design).

Going up from here leads to Floor 5; going down leads to Floor 7.

### Floor 5: Armor Room
A large pillar dominates the room, carved with stone faces on all four sides. Several suits of armor stand alone or are recessed into the walls. When the players enter, one of the suits of armor animates and attacks.

Going up leads to Floor 6; going down leads to Floor 4.

### Floor 6: Music Room
Instruments litter the floor, walls, and tables. Ethereal music plays as the party enters, growing louder and more cacophonous the longer they linger. The music loops a 4-note pattern: 4th, 1st, 2nd, 3rd.

Four cups sit on a table next to a fountain of water, alongside a small metal rod (like one used to play a triangle). The players must fill the cups with varying levels of water and strike them with the rod to produce the correct pitches, unlocking the doors that sealed behind them upon entry.

Going up returns to Floor 2; going down leads to Floor 7.

### Floor 7: Bath / Waterfall Room
Natural foliage lines the walls. A large brazier sits at the center of the room, alongside a large pool fed by a waterfall. The sounds of nature and flowing water fill the air. Several strange trees dotted with blue orbs are scattered throughout.

Going up leads to Floor 3; going down leads to Floor 8.

### Floor 8: Trophy Room
Trophies line the walls — animal pelts and heads, skulls, weapons, statues — alongside two tubs. Two suits of armor stand against a wall, and a large green dragon head dominates the south wall.

Going up leads to Floor 9; going down leads to Floor 1.

### Floor 9: The Final Study
The top of the tower — Chazlauth Yarghorn's personal study, where he can be found studying tomes with his small silver dragon companion.
