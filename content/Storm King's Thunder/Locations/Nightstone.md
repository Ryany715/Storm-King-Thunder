# Nightstone

![[City_Image.png]]

## Overview
A small town that served as the party's entry point into the campaign, Nightstone was found in a state of crisis — its people captured, its ruler dead, and opportunistic outside forces already moving in to fill the power vacuum.

## Unique Features & Landmarks

*   **Description:** The seat of Lady Velrosa's rule.
*   **Significance:** Site where the party discovered Lady Velrosa's body along with several slain guards.

## Surrounding Wilderness Locales

### [[Dripping Caves]]
*   **What it is:** A cave system north of town where the villagers of Nightstone were held captive by goblins.
*   **Why it's untamed/unclaimed:** Home to a goblin warband under Boss Hark, later contested by the Ear-Seekers orc tribe.
*   **Why it's adventure worthy:** Site of a multi-faction battle (goblins vs. orcs vs. players) and Boss Hark's treasure trove.
*   **Notable NPCs/Creatures:** [[Boss Hark]], [[Pojo]], [[Gurrash]], [[Norga One-Eye]]

## Internal Threats

### Goblin & Worg Infestation
*   **Type:** Martial
*   **Manifestation:** Goblins and worgs overran the town, capturing its citizens.
*   **Active Problems:** Villagers taken captive to the Dripping Caves; town guard decimated.
*   **Why hasn't it been dealt with?** Lady Velrosa was killed before she could organize a proper defense.
*   **Key Players:** [[Boss Hark]], [[Pojo]]

### The Zhentarim's Encroachment
*   **Type:** Political / Economic
*   **Manifestation:** Zhentarim agents moved in immediately after the crisis, seeking to convert Nightstone into a trading outpost.
*   **Active Problems:** Long-term political control of the town may shift away from its own leadership.
*   **Why hasn't it been dealt with?** The party did not oppose them, and the town's power structure was too weakened to resist.
*   **Key Players:** [[Kella Darkhope]], [[The Zhentarim]]

## External Threats

### The Ear-Seekers Orc Raid
*   **Type:** Martial
*   **Manifestation:** An orc warband attacked the town shortly after the goblin crisis.
*   **Active Problems:** Nearly resulted in the town being sacked outright.
*   **Why hasn't it been dealt with already?** Redirected toward the Dripping Caves by Snave rather than fully resolved.
*   **Key Players:** [[Gurrash]], [[Norga One-Eye]]

## Plot Hooks
*   The unresolved murder of Lady Velrosa.
*   The long-term consequences of the Zhentarim's foothold in town.
*   Pojo's rule as the new goblin boss at the Dripping Caves — ally or future threat?
