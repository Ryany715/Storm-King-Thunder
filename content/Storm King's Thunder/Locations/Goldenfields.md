# Goldenfields

![[City_Image.png]]

## Overview
A sprawling, walled agricultural hub dedicated to the goddess Chauntea, Goldenfields is a breadbasket of the region — and, in the wake of the Ordning's shattering, a prime target for Brokh the Hill Giant King's escalating raids.

## Unique Features & Landmarks

### 1. The Earth Mother's Bounty
*   **Description:** A stone building with an open front, seen just past the gatehouse.
*   **Significance:** One of the first landmarks the party noticed upon entering the city.
*   **Plot Hooks:** Not yet explored in detail.

### 2. North Furrow's End
*   **Description:** A tavern owned by Miros Xelbrin, also known as the Yeti.
*   **Significance:** Site where the party delivered news of a Xelbrin family death and were rewarded with a magic ring.
*   **Plot Hooks:** Miros's connections and gratitude may prove useful again.

### 3. Harvesthome Abbey
*   **Description:** A quiet abbey home to two old black bears, Darlowe and Tillbee.
*   **Significance:** Discovered by Solliel while the party awaited the council meeting.
*   **Plot Hooks:** Unclear if the abbey or its bears hold further significance yet.

## Surrounding Wilderness Locales

### The Emerald Enclave Grove
*   **What it is:** A grove near Goldenfields home to the druidic Emerald Enclave.
*   **Why it's untamed/unclaimed:** Maintained independently by the Enclave's druids and awakened creatures.
*   **Why it's adventure worthy:** Home to [[Lifferlas]], an awakened treant who has pledged to help rebuild the city's wall, and connected to [[Aerglas]], a renowned giant-killer druid.
*   **Notable NPCs/Creatures:** [[Lifferlas]], [[Turlang]], [[Aerglas]]

## Internal Threats

### Council Discord
*   **Type:** Political
*   **Manifestation:** Tension and accusations among council members, highlighted by Strog Thunderblade's unexplained resistance to a Zone of Truth spell.
*   **Active Problems:** Slows unified decision-making regarding the giant threat.
*   **Why hasn't it been dealt with?** Not yet investigated — Thunderblade's resistance remains unexplained.
*   **Key Players:** [[Strog Thunderblade]], [[Zi Liang]], [[Ellardin]], [[Miros Xelbrin]]

## External Threats

### Brokh the Hill Giant King's Raids
*   **Type:** Martial
*   **Manifestation:** Repeated hill giant, bugbear, and goblin attacks against the city walls and outlying areas.
*   **Active Problems:** A wall breach from a prior attack; an active siege the party helped repel; ongoing threat of further escalation, up to and including a potential siege of Waterdeep itself if unchecked.
*   **Why hasn't it been dealt with already?** Brokh commands a growing horde (his "Skull Crackers") and is driven by an unnatural, magically-cursed hunger and intellect that makes him difficult to reason with or predict.
*   **Key Players:** [[Brokh the Hill Giant King]]

### Homebrew Note: The Defense of Goldenfields (Siege Encounter)
This is the DM's homebrew mechanical breakdown used to resolve the multi-front siege on the city:
*   The players directly fought **2 Hill Giants** and **4 bugbears**.
*   The remaining attackers were split into two other combat groups, resolved abstractly: roll a d20 each round for 5 rounds — a roll of 1–10 marks a point for the ogres (attackers), 11–20 marks a point for the guards (defenders). Whichever side has the majority of marks after 5 rounds wins that combat.
*   The players were assisted by the Captain of the Guard and 3 other guards in their own fight; the two abstracted side-combats were each reinforced by 2 captains and 4 guards.
*   Following the party's victory, Brokh himself was seen on the hill above the city — an emaciated, sinewy hill giant with oily black hair, standing head and shoulders above the other giants — before raising his hand and leading his forces in retreat.
