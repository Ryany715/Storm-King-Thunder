# Emerald Enclave

## Description
*   **Appearance:** Represented by its grove near Goldenfields and its awakened/druidic members.
*   **Personality:** Nature-aligned and protective of the wild spaces near civilization.
*   **Mannerisms:** [Not yet detailed]

## Background
A druidic organization with a grove located near Goldenfields. The party's druid visited the grove and met Lifferlas, an awakened treant, who agreed to help rebuild the Goldenfields wall damaged by a prior hill giant attack. Lifferlas also spoke of Aerglas, the druid who awakened him and a renowned giant-killer, currently on pilgrimage to Shadowtop Cathedral to consult Turlang, the Enclave's oldest treant.

## Motivations & Goals
To protect the natural world and its allies from the encroaching giant threat, including lending direct aid such as wall reconstruction.
