# The Skull Crackers

## Description
*   **Appearance:** A mixed band of hill giants and small folk cannibal ritualists who travel and raid together.
*   **Personality:** Zealous followers of Brokh's philosophy of consuming the minds of the intelligent and civilized.
*   **Mannerisms:** Small folk members are described as "brain-eating" travelers who have adopted Brokh's cannibalistic practices.

## Background
Formed around [[Brokh the Hill Giant King]], the Skull Crackers began as a herd of hill giants Brokh kept fed and organized through his unnatural intellect. Brokh has since expanded his following by first attempting to convert small folk villages to his practice of cannibalism and "intellectual consumption" — those who agree join his herd as human/small-folk ritualists, becoming Skull Crackers themselves. The group now functions as a roving, Mongol-like warband pillaging and corrupting settlements across the Great Plains, including the Goldenfields.

## Motivations & Goals
To spread Brokh's belief that consuming the minds of the intelligent and civilized grants deeper wisdom and broader intellect, expanding their numbers through conquest, corruption, and cannibalistic conversion.
