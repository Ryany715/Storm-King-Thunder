# The Zhentarim

## Description
*   **Appearance:** Represented so far by agents who approached the party outside Nightstone, accompanied by [[Kella Darkhope]].
*   **Personality:** Pragmatic and opportunistic — moved quickly to capitalize on Nightstone's crisis.
*   **Mannerisms:** [Not yet detailed]

## Background
Shortly after the goblin/worg crisis in Nightstone and the death of Lady Velrosa, agents of the Zhentarim — friends of Kella Darkhope — approached the party as they left town, explaining their intention to use the destabilized town as a new trading outpost.
