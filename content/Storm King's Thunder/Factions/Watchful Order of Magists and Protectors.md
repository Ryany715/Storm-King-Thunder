## Motivations & Goals
To provide magical support and protection to allied settlements, and to further its own research into the giant crisis and the shattered Ordning.

The City of Water deep receives much of it's agrarian goods from the Goldenfields and is motivated to ensure that the city stays protected. With proper motivation they could be convinced to provide aid and send help to the cause of protecting the town.
