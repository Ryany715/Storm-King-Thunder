# Watchful Order of Magists and Protectors

## Description
*   **Appearance:** Represented in the field by attachés such as [[Naxene Drathkala]].
*   **Personality:** Scholarly and diplomatically engaged; willing to lend aid when properly petitioned.
*   **Mannerisms:** [Not yet detailed]

## Background
A Waterdeep-based organization of mages and protectors, with an attaché — Naxene Drathkala — stationed in Goldenfields to monitor the region. Following Brokh the Hill Giant King's escalating attacks, the party successfully petitioned the Order (via Naxene and Snave's personal connection to her) for aid from Waterdeep, in the form of battle mages to defend Goldenfields.

## Motivations & Goals
To provide magical support and protection to allied settlements, and to further its own research into the giant crisis and the shattered Ordning.
